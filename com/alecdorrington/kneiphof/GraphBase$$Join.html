trait Join[+V, +E, +L, M] extends GraphBase[V, E | L, M], Join[V, E, L, M]

The join of two graphs, which remembers its new edges so that they can be relabelled with withLabel. Equality compares only the graphs, so equal joins can relabel different edges (see HypergraphBase.Join).

Type parameters

The label of the new edges.

def cartesianProduct[W, F >: E | L](that: HypergraphBase[W, F, M]): HypergraphBase[(V, W), F, M]

Attributes

Returns

The Cartesian product G □ H of this hypergraph and that, on the pairs (v, w) of their vertices, with a copy of each edge of this hypergraph for each vertex w of that, and a copy of each edge of that for each vertex v of this hypergraph. Copies keep their labels. The copies of two loops which differ only in their end coincide, which is one edge, for edge sets. So path(m) □ path(n) is a grid.

override def difference[N](that: HypergraphBase[Any, Any, N])(using embedding: Embedding[N, M]): GraphBase[V, E | L, M]

Attributes

Returns

The difference of this hypergraph and that, which keeps the vertices of this hypergraph and subtracts the multiplicity of each edge in that. So Graph.complete(n) - g is the complement of a Graph[Int, Unit] g on the vertices 0 until n. To remove the vertices of that as well, with their edges, use removeVertices(that.vertexValues). that may be any hypergraph or graph whose multiplicities embed in M, so it may also be an edge or chain, as in g - (1 ~ 2).

def flatMapVertices[W, F >: E | L](transform: V => HypergraphBase[W, F, M]): HypergraphBase[W, F, M]

Attributes

Returns

The hypergraph in which each vertex v is replaced by the hypergraph transform(v), and each edge by its copies between the replacements of its ends. Equal vertices of different replacements merge. Each end is replaced independently, even where a vertex repeats, so a loop at v becomes a copy for every ordered pair of vertices of transform(v): a loop at each, and two copies of the edge between every two (one, for edge sets).

Attributes

Returns

The join of this hypergraph and that, with an extra edge labelled () between every vertex of this hypergraph and every vertex of that, which HypergraphBase.Join.withLabel can relabel. This is the flattening of the edge this ~ that, so it pairs each vertex of this hypergraph with each vertex of that even where the two share vertices: a vertex in both gains a loop, and two vertices in both are joined once for each way of pairing them (which is just once, for edge sets).

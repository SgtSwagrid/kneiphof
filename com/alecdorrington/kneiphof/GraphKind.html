GraphKind

com.alecdorrington.kneiphof.GraphKind
trait GraphKind[M, X <: ([v, e] =>> AnyEdge[v, e, X]), G <: ([v, e] =>> GraphOps[v, e, M, X, G]), C <: ([v, e] =>> GraphOps[v, e, Count, X, C])]()

A kind of graph G, with edges of kind X and multiplicities in M, which builds the graphs of that kind around their incidence structures.

Each kind's companion offers one, such as Graph or GraphBase.kind.

Type parameters

C

The multigraphs of the same kind, such as edges and chains, from which graphs of this kind can be built.

trait Join[+V, +E, +L, M] extends DirectedGraphBase[V, E | L, M], Join[V, E, L, M]

The join of two directed graphs, which remembers its new edges so that they can be relabelled with withLabel. Equality compares only the graphs, so equal joins can relabel different edges (see HypergraphBase.Join).

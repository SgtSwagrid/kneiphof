DirectedEdge

com.alecdorrington.kneiphof.DirectedEdge
See theDirectedEdge companion object
final case class DirectedEdge[+S, +T, +E](source: S, target: T, label: E) extends DirectedHyperedge[S, T, E], AnyEdge[S | T, E, Arc], DirectedGraphBase[S | T, E, Count], DirectedChain[S | T, Nothing, E]

A directed edge from a source of type S to a target of type T.

Usually written source ~> target, or source ~> target % label. An edge is the directed multigraph containing just itself, once, and the DirectedChain of one edge, so source ~> target ~> next adds the edge from target to next.

def cartesianProduct[W, F >: Nothing | E](that: DirectedGraphBase[W, F, Count]): DirectedGraphBase[(S | T, W), F, Count]

Attributes

Returns

The Cartesian product G □ H of this graph and that, on the pairs (v, w) of their vertices, with a copy of each edge of this graph for each vertex w of that, and a copy of each edge of that for each vertex v of this graph. Copies keep their labels. The copies of two loops which differ only in their end coincide, which is one edge, for edge sets. So path(m) □ path(n) is a grid.

Returns

The difference of this hypergraph and that, which keeps the vertices of this hypergraph and subtracts the multiplicity of each edge in that. So DirectedGraph.complete(n) - g is the complement of a DirectedGraph[Int, Unit] g on the vertices 0 until n. To remove the vertices of that as well, with their edges, use removeVertices(that.vertexValues). that may be any directed hypergraph or directed graph whose multiplicities embed in M, so it may also be an edge or chain, as in g - (1 ~> 2).

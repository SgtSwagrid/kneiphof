GraphOps

com.alecdorrington.kneiphof.GraphOps
trait GraphOps[+V, +E, M, +X <: ([v, e] =>> AnyEdge[v, e, X]), +G <: ([v, e] =>> GraphOps[v, e, M, X, G])]() extends AnyGraph[V, E, M]

The operations shared by every kind of graph, which preserve its kind.

Operations which add edges depend on the kind, so they are defined by each kind instead: see HypergraphBase, GraphBase, DirectedHypergraphBase and DirectedGraphBase.
